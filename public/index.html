Privacy Notice

Lily Ava's Silogan respects your privacy and handles your personal information in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

What we collect

Your name, contact number, delivery address and area (for delivery orders), the items you order, your preferred pickup/delivery time, payment method and GCash reference number (if any), and any notes you add.

Why we collect it

Only to receive, prepare, deliver and confirm your order, to contact you about it, and to keep basic sales records. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Only the shop owner and staff handling orders. Your order is stored on our hosting provider (Cloudflare) and may be sent to you through WhatsApp or SMS if you choose to message us.

How long we keep it

Only as long as needed to fulfil your order and for our business records, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, by contacting us at 0966 613 8689.
